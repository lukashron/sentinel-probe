**purpose of use**, not on who you are.

under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Noncommercial use includes in particular:

- personal use, testing your own hobby projects, study and teaching,
- academic and independent security research without commercial application,
- use by educational institutions, public research organizations, charities,

When distributing the software, you must also pass on the license text and the
`Required Notice` line from the `LICENSE` file.

## What counts as commercial use

You need a commercial license if you use Sentinel Probe for profit or as part of
a business activity, for example if you:

- test websites or applications of your company or employer,
- provide paid services (penetration testing, security audits, website administration)
  and use Sentinel Probe while doing so,
- incorporate Sentinel Probe or any part of it into your own product or service (including SaaS),
- use it as a self-employed person or freelancer when working for clients.
